STOCKYARD PAY AHEAD DEMO - HOW TO PUT IT ON AN IPHONE OR IPAD
================================================================

The "app" folder is a small website: the demo page, its icon and its app settings.
To save it on an iPhone home screen with the icon, it has to be on the web first.
The free, no-account-needed way:

1. On your computer, go to  https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Drag the whole "app" folder onto that page.
3. Netlify gives you a web address (something like https://random-name.netlify.app).
   (Make a free account if you want that address to stay up for good.)
4. On the iPhone or iPad, open that address in Safari.
5. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow), then "Add to Home Screen".
6. It shows up as "Pay Ahead" with the blue phone-and-checkmark icon,
   and opens full screen like a normal app.

Other files in this folder
--------------------------
- pay-ahead-demo.html ............ the main source file (edit this one)
- Stockyard Pay Ahead Demo (standalone).html ... one-file copy you can email or AirDrop
                                   and open in any browser (needs internet for fonts and 3D)
- build_app.py ................... after editing pay-ahead-demo.html, run
                                   "python build_app.py" to refresh the app folder
                                   and the standalone copy

Changing names, prescriptions or prices
---------------------------------------
Open pay-ahead-demo.html in a text editor and find "Demo settings" near the top
of the script. The pharmacy name, prescriptions, prices, cards and sample
patients are listed there.
